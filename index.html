Growing taller: what is real

  • Your final height is mostly genetics. No exercise, stretch, pill or gadget adds height.
  • What you can control is reaching the height your genes allow:
    • Sleep 8 to 10 hours. Most growth hormone releases during deep sleep.
    • Eat enough, including protein at every meal, plus calcium and vitamin D foods (dairy or fortified alternatives, eggs, fish, greens).
    • Stay active. Lifting with good form is safe for growing bodies.
  • Skip growth supplements and "height hacks". They do nothing and some are harmful.
  • Ask a pediatrician about your growth chart. A simple hand X-ray (bone age) shows how much growing time is left.

    Safety rules built in

    • No calorie targets, fasting, or weight-loss deadlines. Ever.
    • Never more than 6 hard days in a row. Rest is part of the plan.
    • Start with the easier version of a move. Add reps before adding load.
    • Sharp pain, dizziness or chest pain means stop and tell an adult.
    • Loop in a parent and a pediatrician for anything about weight or nutrition.

    Stride is education, not medical advice.
